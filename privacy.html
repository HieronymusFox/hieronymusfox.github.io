Privacy Policy

Hieronymus Fox games · Effective September 16, 2026

This policy explains how Hieronymus Fox handles information in our games.

Information We Collect

Our games do not require an account with us and do not include advertising, analytics or tracking. Your progress and settings are saved on your device, and we do not have access to them. We receive personal information only if you contact us, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

Stores and Platforms

Our games are distributed through Steam, the Microsoft Store, the Amazon Appstore and itch.io. These services handle your account, purchases and platform features such as achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves under their own privacy policies. Leaderboards may show your platform profile name and score to other players. Android device backups are handled by Google. Our games may confirm a purchase with the store, but never receive your payment details.

How We Use Information

We use the information you send us to respond to your request, provide support and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and Transfers

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We disclose it only to service providers that help us receive and answer support requests, or when required by law. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries.

Retention and Security

We keep support messages only as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect them.

Your Choices and Rights

You can remove saved game data from your device at any time and manage platform features in that platform's settings. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or receive a copy of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To make a request, email us. For information held by a store or platform, contact that service.

Children's Privacy

Our games do not ask players for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by law in their country. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can address it as required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will always be posted at this address.
